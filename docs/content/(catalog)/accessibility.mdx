The accessibility button runs [axe-core](https://github.com/dequelabs/axe-core) inside the preview and lists what it reports: the impact, a description, a link to learn more, and the elements involved.

- The reported elements are outlined in the preview in the color of their impact. Hover or focus an element in the list to draw it thicker, or click it to keep it that way and scroll to it. An element inside a shadow root is listed as its host's selector, `>>>`, then its selector inside
- What axe-core cannot decide on its own, such as the contrast of text over an image or a gradient, is listed separately under "Needs a manual check" and outlined with a dashed line. Read about each rule (Learn more) and check it yourself: some are visual, such as contrast, and some are not, such as an ARIA attribute. When nothing else is found, the panel says so instead of reporting no issues
- The check runs again each time the preview reloads after an edit, while the panel is open
- "Simulate vision" shows the preview as seen with blurred vision or a color vision deficiency (protanopia, deuteranopia, tritanopia, achromatopsia). Each chip shows a sample of its colors
- With `locale: 'ja'`, axe-core's messages are in Japanese too

axe-core is loaded only when a check runs. The rule about landmarks around all page content is off, since a component on its own has none.
