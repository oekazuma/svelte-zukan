"zukan" (図鑑) is Japanese for "illustrated encyclopedia".

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="One line in vite.config.ts" icon="plug">
    Add `zukan()` to your Vite plugins. No routes or config files in `src`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Next to your components" icon="folder">
    Put `Button.zukan.svelte` and `Button.zukan.md` next to `Button.svelte` and they show up in the catalog.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Preview and code" icon="code">
    Each section previews in a resizable iframe, and its code is shown with the variables it uses.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Props and accessibility" icon="accessibility">
    A props table read from the component, and axe-core checks with outlined issues and vision simulation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Phone to desktop widths" icon="smartphone">
    Presets from 375px to 1280px. Narrow widths are centered, wide ones scaled to fit your screen.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Static export" icon="package">
    With `build: true`, `vite build` also writes a static catalog that works under any subpath.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
